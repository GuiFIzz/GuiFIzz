# Offer and messaging

## Positioning

**Metabolic Flex.** A coaching system that uses **every tool available now**
(GLP-1s, hormone therapy, Lumen metabolic tracking, training, nutrition) to get
you metabolically healthy, **and** builds the behavior that eventually replaces
the tools.

- Most GLP-1 clinics sell a prescription, which people stop, and then the weight
  comes back.
- Most coaches refuse to talk about medication at all.
- **We do both: tools to start, behavior to finish.** The tools are the bridge
  and the behavior is where you end up.

Tagline options (A/B test):
1. *Use every tool. Keep only the habits.*
2. *Medication gets you started. Behavior keeps you there.*
3. *Keep your muscle. Lose the dysfunction.*
4. *Know when to eat, not just what to eat.* (a Lumen angle)
5. *Discipline. Accountability. Consistency.* (the coach's core promise)

### The Lumen offer
- **Headline:** "Free Lumen metabolic device ($249 value) when you join."
- **Lumen membership:** the first 12 months are included with the device, so there's no app cost during the program.
- **Cost to GFC Xtreme:** $199 per client. It's covered in month one ($299 coaching) and fully protected by the

  agent watches for missed logs and nudges. **Adherence data = retention**, and retention past the 3-month minimum
  is where most of the profit is.

## Voice

- Direct, warm, coach-in-your-corner. Not clinical, not bro.
- Talk about **metabolism, energy, muscle, habits**, not "diets."
- Never shame. The enemy is the dysfunction, not the person.
- Always pair a tool with a behavior: "GLP-1 + protein + lifting," "Lumen + meal timing."

## Core message bank (reused by Content, Ads and Nurture agents)

1. **The muscle problem.** Up to a third or more of the weight lost on GLP-1s can be lean mass if you don't train and eat
   protein. Losing muscle slows your metabolism. *(cite a source in the post; see guardrails)*
2. **Tools are a bridge.** Medication is a tool. So is a Lumen. The destination is habits you own.
3. **Know when to eat.** Your breath can tell you if you're burning fat or carbs this morning. Use that to plan meals.
4. **Every two weeks, someone checks.** Accountability is the thing most programs are missing.
5. **Metabolic dysfunction is common.** A large majority of adults show at least one marker. You're not broken; you're normal in a broken environment.
6. **Pre-diabetes is a window.** It's the best time to act. Lifestyle programs cut progression risk substantially (the DPP study).
