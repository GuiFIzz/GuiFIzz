## Original full question list (reference)

Every default in this repo is a placeholder. Your answers turn it into your
real system. Answer what you can; I've listed **why each answer matters**.
The top block matters most.

## A. Money: what is a client worth? (drives ad budget)

1. **Price.** What do you charge for coaching? Monthly, or a package (3, 6, 12 months)?
   *Why:* sets lifetime value, and lifetime value sets how much we can pay for a lead.
2. **Retention.** How many months does a typical client stay today? If you're new, what's your guess?
3. **Lumen cost.** What does one Lumen device cost you (wholesale/partner price)? Does the client
   pay the Lumen app subscription, or do you?
   *Why:* a free device is the strongest hook in the funnel and also the biggest cost per
   client. It must be tied to a commitment (e.g. 6 months), or we lose money on people who quit.
4. **Telehealth revenue.** Do you earn on GLP-1 or hormone therapy (consult fee, membership,
   medication margin)? Who prescribes: you, a partner physician, or a platform
   (e.g. OpenLoop, Wheel, Beluga)?
5. **Monthly ad budget** to start: $500? $1,500? $5,000?

## B. Capacity: how many people can you coach?

6. How many coaching hours per week do you want? How long is each biweekly session (30/45/60 min)?
7. 1:1 only, or also small groups (like a monthly "Power Warrior" group call)? Groups let you
   coach many more people at the same quality.
8. Which days/times can clients book discovery calls?

## C. Who we target

9. Who are your best current or past clients (age, sex, job, main problem)? Three examples beat any persona I invent.
10. Where do you practice? Which **US states** (or countries) can you legally serve for
    telehealth? Coaching can be anywhere, prescriptions can't.
11. Languages: English only, or also Portuguese/Spanish? (That could be a big underserved channel.)
12. Should the diabetic / pre-diabetic "Power Warrior" program be its own funnel
    (separate ads, quiz and emails)? I recommend yes.

## D. Proof and assets

13. Do you have client results (before/after, A1c drops, weight, waist) with **written
    permission** to use them?
14. Credentials to feature: certifications, degrees, years, gym (GFC Xtreme?).
15. Existing accounts: Instagram, Facebook page, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, Google Business
    Profile, website/domain, email list, CRM, booking tool? Anything with followers or contacts
    counts as an asset.
16. Can you record **one 60-second phone video per week**? Faces sell coaching and AI avatars don't.

## E. Rules

17. Any claims you don't want made ("reverse diabetes", "lose X lbs")? (I've already blocked
    several; see `04-compliance-guardrails.md`.)
18. Who signs off on medical content: you, or a clinician partner?
19. Should the system recommend spend increases, or should spending scale automatically
    within limits you set (e.g. "never over $X/day")?
