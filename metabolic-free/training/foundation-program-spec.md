# Metabolic Flex — 12-week training program spec

The real training content behind Phase 1. Replaces the `GFCxtreme Bodyweight SED 1 / SED 2`
placeholder groups currently attached to all three packages.

Built on the program already described publicly on the site: 3 full-body strength sessions a
week, progressing Foundation → Build → Strength across 12 weeks, with daily habits layered on
top (morning Lumen breath, protein at every meal, 10-minute walk after the biggest meal).

---

## ⚠ Blocker: the exercise library is press-dominant

The current custom library is 41 movements and contains **no back work at all**.

| Pattern | Count in library |
|---|---|
| Horizontal push (chest) | 4 |
| Vertical push (shoulders) | 9 |
| Triceps isolation | 10 |
| Biceps isolation | 2 |
| Squat / lunge / quad | 15 |
| **Horizontal pull (row)** | **0** |
| **Vertical pull (pulldown / pull-up)** | **0** |
| **Hip hinge / posterior chain** | **1** (Dumbbell Swing) |
| **Core** | 0 |

"Barbell Trap Raise (Upright Row)" is an upper-trap and lateral-delt movement, not a back pull.

A 12-week full-body program written from this library alone would be roughly 25 pressing
movements against zero pulls. That produces anterior-dominant shoulder loading and the exact
posture problems the program claims to prevent, and it leaves the lats and mid-back — a large
share of upper-body lean mass — untrained, which works directly against the "muscle is your
metabolic organ" argument the science page is built on.

### Minimum additions to film (6 movements × 3 variants = 18 videos)

| # | Movement | Pattern | Fills |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dumbbell One-Arm Row | horizontal pull | lats, rhomboids, mid-traps |
| 2 | Lat Pulldown *or* Assisted Pull-Up | vertical pull | lats, back width |
| 3 | Romanian Deadlift (DB or barbell) | hip hinge | hamstrings, glutes, erectors |
| 4 | Seated / Lying Leg Curl | knee flexion | hamstrings |
| 5 | Face Pull *or* Rear Delt Fly | horizontal pull, high | posterior delts, external rotators |
| 6 | Plank *or* Dead Bug | anti-extension | deep core |

Follow the standing 3-variant workflow: confirm the movement from real footage first, then
produce **breathing**, **tempo** and **common mistake** cuts for each.

**Interim option:** PT Distinction's stock PTD library (~1,200 movements) covers all six. Using
stock for the pull patterns gets a sound program live immediately, at the cost of breaking the
"every exercise filmed with a real human — your coach" promise for those six slots. Recommend
stock only as a bridge, with his own footage swapped in as it's filmed.

Everything below is written against the **full** list (existing 41 + the 6 above) and is ready
to load the moment those are available. Movements marked ⚠ are the ones not yet filmed.

## Session structure

- **3 sessions a week**, non-consecutive days (Mon / Wed / Fri pattern)
- Each session is **full body**: one squat pattern, one push, one pull, one hinge or
  accessory, plus isolation
- **Warm-up:** 5 minutes easy cardio + 1 light set of the first movement
- **Rest:** 60–90 s on isolation, 2 min on compounds
- Tempo is written as **eccentric–pause–concentric** (e.g. `3-1-1` = 3 s down, 1 s pause, 1 s up).
  The library's tempo-variant videos exist for exactly this — point clients at them in weeks 1–4.

---

## Phase 1 · FOUNDATION — weeks 1–4
**Goal:** learn the movement patterns, build work capacity, install the daily habits.
**Intensity:** RPE 6–7. Leave 3–4 reps in reserve. Load is secondary to control.
**Emphasis:** tempo. This is the phase the tempo videos were made for.

### Session A
| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Tempo | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Goblet Squat | 3 × 10 | 3-1-1 | Upright torso, knees tracking toes |
| 2 | Push-Up *(or Barbell Chest Press)* | 3 × 10 | 2-1-1 | Scale to incline if needed |
| 3 | ⚠ Dumbbell One-Arm Row | 3 × 10 / side | 2-1-2 | Flat back, no torso rotation |
| 4 | Dumbbell Shoulder Press | 2 × 12 | 2-0-1 | Ribs down, no lumbar arch |
| 5 | ⚠ Romanian Deadlift | 2 × 10 | 3-0-1 | Hinge, not squat. Soft knees |
| 6 | ⚠ Plank | 3 × 30 s | — | Glutes on, ribs down |

### Session B
| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Tempo | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Leg Press | 3 × 12 | 3-1-1 | Full range, no lumbar rounding |
| 2 | Dumbbell Incline Chest Press | 3 × 10 | 2-1-1 | Upper-chest emphasis |
| 3 | ⚠ Lat Pulldown | 3 × 12 | 2-1-2 | Lead with elbows, chest tall |
| 4 | Walking Lunges | 2 × 10 / leg | controlled | Back knee toward floor |
| 5 | Seated Calf Raise | 2 × 15 | 2-1-2 | Full stretch at the bottom |
| 6 | Barbell Bicep Curl | 2 × 12 | 2-0-1 | Elbows fixed at the sides |

### Session C
| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Tempo | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Barbell Squat | 3 × 10 | 3-1-1 | Start light, groove the pattern |
| 2 | Chest Fly | 2 × 12 | 3-1-1 | Soft elbows, big stretch |
| 3 | ⚠ Seated Cable Row | 3 × 12 | 2-1-2 | Shoulders down, squeeze mid-back |
| 4 | Dumbbell Lateral Raise | 2 × 12 | 2-0-2 | Lead with the elbow |
| 5 | Tricep Rope Pushdown | 2 × 12 | 2-0-1 | Elbows pinned |
| 6 | Dumbbell Swing | 3 × 12 | explosive | Hip drive, not a squat |

**Weeks 1–2:** learn the patterns, deliberately conservative loads, watch the tempo video before
each new movement.
**Weeks 3–4:** same movements, same sets/reps, add load where all reps were completed with clean
form and tempo held.

---

## Phase 2 · BUILD — weeks 5–8
**Goal:** add load and volume. Hypertrophy bias.
**Intensity:** RPE 7–8. 2–3 reps in reserve.
**Change from Foundation:** rep range drops slightly, sets increase, tempo relaxes on compounds
so load can rise.

- Compounds move to **4 × 8–10**, tempo `2-0-1`
- Isolation stays **3 × 12**
- Add one set to the first two movements of each session
- Progression: add load when the top of the rep range is hit on all sets with clean form

**Substitutions to keep it fresh without new motor learning:**
- Goblet Squat → Barbell Squat with 3-Second Hold
- Push-Up → Barbell Chest Press
- Walking Lunges → Barbell Lunges
- Dumbbell Shoulder Press → Dumbbell Arnold Press
- Tricep Rope Pushdown → Overhead Tricep Rope Extension

---

## Phase 3 · STRENGTH — weeks 9–12
**Goal:** heavier, fewer reps. Maximum stimulus to retain lean mass.
**Intensity:** RPE 8. 1–2 reps in reserve on main lifts.

- Main lift each session: **4 × 5–6**, tempo `2-0-X` (X = fast intent on the way up), rest 2–3 min
- Secondary compound: **3 × 8**
- Isolation: **2 × 12**, kept light — the job here is recovery, not fatigue
- **Week 12 is a deload:** same movements, 60% of the load, 2 sets each. Retest and re-measure.

**Main lifts by session:** A = Barbell Squat · B = Barbell Chest Press · C = ⚠ Romanian Deadlift

## Daily habits (all 12 weeks)

These run alongside every session and are what the daily 60-second check-in tracks:

1. **Morning Lumen breath** — before food or coffee. Carbs get timed to the reading.
2. **Protein at every meal** — ~0.7–1 g per lb of goal bodyweight per day.
3. **Vegetables and protein first, carbs last** — same food, different order.
4. **10-minute walk after the biggest meal.**
5. **Seven hours of sleep.**

## Scope and safety

This is a general strength template, not an individualised prescription. It assumes a healthy
adult cleared for exercise. Per-client adjustment for injury, limitation, training age and
goal happens at onboarding and in the biweekly call — which is the stated reason the SED
placeholders existed in the first place.

Clients on a GLP-1 follow the same structure: the strength work and the protein target are
what protect lean mass while the medication suppresses appetite. Medication decisions stay
with the GFC Lab clinician.
