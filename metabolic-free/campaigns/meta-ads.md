# Meta ads (Facebook + Instagram)

**Objective:** Leads (website conversion on quiz submit) → later Schedule.
**Structure:** 1 campaign (CBO) → 3 ad sets (one per angle) → 2–3 creatives each.
**Audience:** broad, age 35–65, target states/regions only. Let creative do the targeting.
(Health-condition interest targeting is largely removed by Meta, so don't rely on it.)
**Budget (Phase 1):** $30–50/day total.
**Link:** `https://<domain>/?utm_source=meta&utm_campaign={{campaign.name}}&utm_content={{ad.id}}`

All copy passes `docs/04-compliance-guardrails.md`: no "you are diabetic", no drug brand names, no guaranteed results.

---

## Angle 1: Keep your muscle (GLP-1 wedge, no drug names)

**Video hook (coach on camera, 0–3 s):** "If you're losing weight fast, check this: how much of it is muscle?"

**Primary text:**
> Losing weight is easy to measure. Losing *muscle* isn't, and it's what slows your metabolism down later.
>
> Whether you're using medication, dieting, or just starting, the plan is the same: protein, strength, and
> knowing when your body is burning fat.
>
> Take the 2-minute Metabolic Score and see where you stand. 👇

**Headline:** Lose fat. Keep your muscle.  **CTA:** Learn more

## Angle 2: Power Warrior (blood sugar)

**Hook:** "Told your blood sugar is 'a little high'? That's not a sentence. It's a window."

**Primary text:**
> "Borderline" is the best time to act. Research shows structured lifestyle programs can significantly lower the
> chance of progressing.
>
> Power Warrior is a coached program: training, nutrition timing, a metabolic device that reads your breath,
> and a check-in with your coach every two weeks. It works alongside your doctor, not instead of them.
>
> Start with the free Metabolic Score. 👇

**Headline:** Take control of your blood sugar markers  **CTA:** Learn more

## Angle 3: Know when to eat (Lumen)

**Hook:** (Coach breathing into the Lumen) "This tells me if I'm burning fat or carbs right now."

**Primary text:**
> Stop guessing. Each morning a breath shows whether your body is running on fat or carbs, and that tells you
> what to eat today.
>
> Our coaching clients get a **Lumen device free** with their program, plus a coach who reads the data with them
> every two weeks.
>
> See if you qualify. Take the Metabolic Score. 👇

**Headline:** Free metabolic device with coaching  **CTA:** See if you qualify

## Angle 4 (test later): Every tool, then only habits
**Hook:** "Medication, hormones, apps... use them. Then make yourself not need them."

## Retargeting (Phase 2)
- Quiz starters who didn't finish: "Your Metabolic Score is 60 seconds from done."
- Leads who didn't book: coach video "What happens on a discovery call" + a client testimonial (consented).
