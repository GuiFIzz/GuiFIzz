# The Metabolic Flex System — blueprint film

Production blueprint for the GFC Xtreme flagship system film. Structure modelled on the
QINV "Advisor OS" reference video: a numbered, stage-by-stage walkthrough of a proprietary
system, with a persistent progress breadcrumb, one bold claim per stage, and cinematic
inserts between stages.

**Purpose:** show that GFC Xtreme is not a trainer with a workout plan. It is an operating
system for metabolic health — measurable inputs, clinical oversight, and a human holding
the line — aimed at maximum healthy longevity.

---

## 1 · What we borrowed from the reference

| Reference device | How we use it |
|---|---|
| Persistent breadcrumb across the top, current stage lit | `MEASURE › MOVE › FUEL › TEST › ADJUST › SUSTAIN` |
| Tiny numbered section label (`2 · CONSOLIDAÇÃO`) | `02 · MOVE` in letterspaced caps, accent orange |
| One short, declarative headline per stage | "The number one anti-aging variable." |
| Floating product UI cards in 3D perspective on near-black | PT Distinction app, Lumen app, lab panel, progress charts |
| Pill tags along the bottom naming the sub-components | `3× / week` `progressive load` `demo videos` |
| Big single-stat moments (`8+ anos`, `146+ agentes`) | `~80%` glucose uptake · `26` coaching sessions a year |
| Cinematic insert between stages (data centre, skyline) | Cellular/mitochondrial render, dark gym, sunrise coast |
| Closing three-pillar card | **Discipline · Accountability · Consistency** |

**Deliberate difference:** the reference is pure product UI and voice-free text. Ours opens
and closes on a real human face with real voice, because the product *is* the coach. Software
is the middle of our film, not the whole of it.

## 2 · Visual system

- **Ground:** near-black `#0A0A0A`, subtle perspective grid floor, atmospheric haze
- **Accent:** GFC orange `#F26B2A` — used sparingly: the lit breadcrumb step, one key number, the CTA
- **Type:** heavy sans headline, left-aligned, generous negative space; tiny letterspaced caps for labels
- **Motion:** slow push-ins and lateral parallax only. No whip pans, no bounce. Calm = credible.
- **Logo:** GFC Xtreme mark bottom-left persistent at low opacity; full-screen on the end card
- **Aspect:** master 9:16 for paid social; 16:9 re-frame for YouTube/site hero

## 3 · The six stages

### 00 · OPEN
> **"Most programs hand you a workout. We hand you a system."**

- **Visual:** hero — coach alone in a dark gym, orange rim light, haze, heavy negative space above

### 01 · MEASURE — "Know the starting point."
What we actually do before changing anything.

- Metabolic Score — 2-minute self-assessment, screens the warning signs
- Lumen baseline — a morning breath that shows fat vs. carb burn
- Full blood panel ordered through GFC Lab

**Pills:** `metabolic score` · `lumen baseline` · `blood panel`
**Stat card:** `~80%` — *share of post-meal glucose taken up by skeletal muscle*
**Visual:** lab bench / blood tubes insert, floating score card
**VO:** *"We measure first. A metabolic score, a morning breath reading, and a real blood panel. No guessing about where you're starting."*

---

### 02 · MOVE — "The number one anti-aging variable."
Non-negotiable. This is the stage that protects everything else.

- 3 full-body strength sessions a week
- 12 weeks structured Foundation → Build → Strength
- Every exercise filmed with a real human, with its own breathing cue and tempo cue

**Pills:** `3× / week` · `progressive load` · `human demo videos`
**Visual:** goblet squat, dark gym, orange rim light
**VO:** *"One: we keep you moving. Strength training is the number one anti-aging variable there is. This one is non-negotiable."*

---

### 03 · FUEL — "Know what to eat, and when."
Nutrition as a measured input, not a diet.

- Free Lumen device ($249 value) with 12 months membership
- Coached on how to read it — carbs timed to what you're actually burning
- Protein-first, vegetables second, carbs last
- Protein target set to protect lean mass
- 10-minute walk after the biggest meal

**Pills:** `lumen-guided` · `protein first` · `post-meal walk`
**Visual:** coach taking the morning breath reading at the kitchen table; cut to Lumen + app + meal
**VO:** *"Two: we teach you how, when and what to eat. Every client gets a free Lumen, and I coach you how to use it — so you protect your number one metabolic tool: your muscle."*

### 04 · TEST — "Decisions from your numbers, not guesswork."
Where coaching stops and medicine starts — and we are explicit about the line.

- Licensed clinicians at GFC Lab review your panel
- GLP-1s, peptides, hormone therapy, supplementation — **only when appropriate, prescribed by them, not by your coach**
- Training and protein built around the protocol from day one so what you lose is fat

**Pills:** `GLP-1` · `peptides` · `hormones` · `supplements`
**Stat card:** `25–40%` — *share of weight lost that can be lean mass without a strength plan*
**Visual:** clinician reviewing blood panel on tablet, sample tubes foreground
**VO:** *"Three: blood work. Our certified doctors and clinicians at GFC Lab read your panel and guide what you actually need — based on your numbers, not guesswork."*

---

### 05 · ADJUST — "Someone is actually watching."
The accountability layer — the part that makes the other four stick.

- 60-second daily check-in, reviewed by your coach
- Direct messaging with the coach inside the platform
- 30-minute 1:1 call every two weeks

**Pills:** `daily check-in` · `direct messaging` · `biweekly call`
**Stat card:** `26` — *coaching sessions a year*
**Visual:** coaching desk, laptop, progress notebook — coach speaking to camera
**VO:** *"Four: accountability. Direct messaging with me inside the platform, plus a thirty-minute call every two weeks."*

### 06 · SUSTAIN — "Need the tools less."
The actual destination, and the thing almost nobody else says out loud.

- Metabolic flexibility restored — your body switches fuels the way it should
- Tools tapered **with your clinician** as behaviour takes over
- The habits are what remain

**Pills:** `taper` · `habits` · `longevity`
**Visual:** cellular/mitochondrial render → sunrise coastal walk
**VO:** *"Then we make you need the tools less. That's the whole point. Use every tool — keep only the habits."*

## 5 · Production notes

- **Generate VO per stage, not per film.** Seedance caps reference audio around 20 s; every
  stage script above is written to land inside that. Longer takes fail submission silently
  with a 422.
- **Typography is not AI-generated.** The breadcrumb, pill tags, stat cards and headlines
  must be built in the edit (or as HTML/CSS screenshots) — image models mangle small text.
  Generate clean plates, add type in post.
- **Product UI:** the reference floats real app screenshots. Use real PT Distinction and
  Lumen app captures, not invented interfaces — a fake UI undermines the credibility the
  whole film is built on.
- **Lip-sync only where he speaks to camera** (00 OPEN, 05 ADJUST, CLOSE). Everywhere else
  the voice is narration over b-roll; prompt explicitly that the subject's mouth stays closed.
- **Compliance line to hold:** clinicians prescribe, the coach does not. Never state or imply
  the program treats, reverses or cures a disease. "May help support" is the ceiling. Keep
  "individual results vary" on any outcome claim.
