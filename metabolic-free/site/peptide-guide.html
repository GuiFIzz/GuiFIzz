Before you read further

  • Nothing here is a recommendation to use any specific peptide. It's background, so your conversation with GFC Lab's clinicians starts from an informed place.
  • Several peptides below are FDA-approved prescription medications for specific uses. Most others are compounded or still in clinical trials — they are not FDA-approved for the general uses discussed in the field, and claims about them are based on early research, not established medical consensus.
  • Dosing, stacking and self-administration decisions should never be made from an article. They belong to a licensed clinician who has evaluated you.
  • If you want to explore whether any of this is appropriate for you, book a GFC Lab visit — that's where the real decision gets made.

Metabolic & weight management

Semaglutide

A GLP-1 receptor agonist — FDA-approved for type 2 diabetes and, at higher doses, chronic weight management. Slows digestion and signals fullness to the brain.

Tirzepatide

A dual GLP-1/GIP receptor agonist, FDA-approved for type 2 diabetes and weight management. Targets both insulin response and appetite regulation.

Retatrutide

Investigational — not yet FDA-approved. A triple GLP-1/GIP/glucagon agonist currently in Phase 3 trials, studied for weight management and metabolic health.

AOD-9604

A modified fragment of growth hormone studied for its role in fat metabolism, without the broader effects of full growth hormone.

5-Amino-1MQ

A small molecule studied for its effect on fat-storage signaling and lean mass preservation during weight loss.

Growth hormone support

Tesamorelin

A growth hormone-releasing hormone (GHRH) analog, FDA-approved for a specific indication (reduction of excess abdominal fat in some patients). Signals the pituitary to release the body's own stored growth hormone, rather than supplying it directly.

Recovery & tissue repair

BPC-157

A compounded peptide studied for its role in blood flow and tissue repair signaling, commonly discussed in the context of soft-tissue and gut-lining recovery. Not FDA-approved.

TB-500 (Thymosin Beta-4)

A compounded peptide studied for its role in cell migration and tissue repair signaling. Not FDA-approved.

Mitochondrial & cellular energy

MOTS-c

A mitochondrial-derived peptide studied for its role in glucose metabolism and exercise-like metabolic signaling. Not FDA-approved.

SS-31

A compound studied for its role in supporting mitochondrial membrane function and reducing oxidative stress. Not FDA-approved.

NAD+

A coenzyme central to cellular energy production and DNA repair pathways, often discussed in longevity and energy-support contexts.

Immune & inflammatory support

Thymosin Alpha-1

A naturally occurring immune-regulating peptide, studied for its role in T-cell function and immune balance. Approved in some countries for specific conditions; not FDA-approved in the U.S. for general use.

KPV

A tripeptide studied for its anti-inflammatory signaling, often discussed in the context of gut and skin inflammation. Not FDA-approved.

Gut health

Larazotide acetate

A synthetic peptide studied for its role in intestinal barrier (tight junction) regulation, primarily researched for celiac disease. Not FDA-approved for general use.

Cognitive & mood support

Selank

A compounded peptide, delivered as a nasal spray, studied for effects on anxiety and cognitive function. Not FDA-approved.

Semax

A compounded peptide studied for neuroprotective and cognitive-support effects. Not FDA-approved.

Antioxidant & detoxification support

Glutathione

A naturally occurring tripeptide antioxidant, central to the body's detoxification pathways. Available as a supplement and, in some clinical settings, as an injectable.

Skin, anti-aging & longevity

GHK-Cu

A naturally occurring copper peptide studied for its role in collagen production and skin repair signaling.

Epitalon

A synthetic peptide studied in longevity research for its effects on cellular aging markers. Not FDA-approved.

Hormonal & libido support

PT-141 (Bremelanotide)

FDA-approved (as Vyleesi) for a specific indication in women. Works through brain pathways involved in sexual arousal, rather than blood flow directly.

Kisspeptin & HCG

Peptides studied for their role in stimulating the body's own reproductive hormone axis, sometimes discussed alongside hormone therapy and fertility support.
