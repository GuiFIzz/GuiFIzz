How I coach

DisciplineA clear weekly plan: three strength sessions, protein at every meal, and a walk after your biggest meal.
AccountabilityA 1:1 session with me every two weeks, plus a 60-second daily check-in I read.
ConsistencySmall habits, repeated, measured by your Lumen each morning, until you need the tools less.

What a session looks like

  1. Your data first: we review your Lumen trend, check-ins, weight and training log from the last two weeks.
  2. What worked, what didn't: we keep what's working and fix one thing at a time.
  3. Your next two weeks: training loads, protein target and meal timing, adjusted to your numbers.
  4. Tools check: if you use a GLP-1, peptides or hormone therapy through GFC Lab, we build around it so you keep your muscle and burn the fat.
