# LinkedIn: compliant B2B + personal brand (no scraping)

## Why B2B
Employers pay for metabolic health because diabetes and obesity drive health-insurance costs. **One HR contract
= 10–50 clients.** LinkedIn is the right place for this; it's the wrong place to find individual patients.

## Target list (built manually or with Sales Navigator)
- Titles: HR Director, Head of People, Benefits Manager, Wellness Coordinator, Owner/CEO (companies of 20–200)
- Industries with sedentary work + older workforce: trucking/logistics, finance, law firms, call centers, government contractors, schools
- Geography: coach's region first (in-person lunch-and-learn is a powerful door-opener)

## Offer: "Metabolic Health Pilot"
- Free 30-min lunch-and-learn: "Energy, blood sugar and muscle after 40"
- Everyone takes the Metabolic Score (anonymous aggregate report to HR, individual results only to each employee; never share individual health data with the employer)
- Paid pilot: 10 employees × 3 months at a group rate

## Message templates (sent by the coach, max 20/day)

**Connection request (≤ 300 chars):**
> Hi {{first_name}}, I coach professionals on metabolic health (energy, blood sugar, muscle after 40). I work with
> teams in {{region}} and would like to connect.

**Follow-up after accept:**
> Thanks for connecting, {{first_name}}. Quick question: is employee energy or rising health-plan cost on your
> radar this year? I run a free 30-min lunch-and-learn on metabolic health that teams like. Happy to send the
> outline, no pitch.

**Follow-up +7 days:**
> Sharing the outline in case it's useful: {{link}}. If a pilot isn't a fit, no worries at all.

## Personal-brand posting (2×/week, repurposed from coach videos)
- A story from coaching (anonymized) + a lesson
- A stat + opinion ("Most GLP-1 programs ignore muscle. Here's why that's a problem for employers too.")
