## The 28 entries

Legend: **P** = primary movers · **S** = secondary movers · ⟲ = unilateral, Notes must say both sides.

### Lower body

**1. Bodyweight Bench Squat** — `IMG_0034`
Sit back to a bench and stand again under control, arms counterbalancing in front. Teaches depth
and a true hip hinge without load.
**P** quadriceps, gluteus maximus · **S** hamstrings, adductors, erector spinae, core

**2. Single-Leg Bench Squat** ⟲ — `IMG_0037`
Lower to the bench on one leg, the other extended forward, then drive back to standing on the
same leg. Exposes and corrects side-to-side strength differences. **Work both sides evenly.**
**P** quadriceps, gluteus maximus · **S** gluteus medius, hamstrings, adductors, core

**3. Bodyweight Squat** — `IMG_0038`
Free-standing squat to parallel, arms forward, chest tall. The base pattern everything else
builds on.
**P** quadriceps, gluteus maximus · **S** hamstrings, adductors, erector spinae, calves

**4. Walking Lunge** — `IMG_0059`
Step forward into a lunge and drive through to the next step, alternating legs continuously.
Trains single-leg strength plus balance in motion.
**P** quadriceps, gluteus maximus · **S** hamstrings, gluteus medius, adductors, calves, core

**5. Bodyweight Reverse Lunge** — `IMG_0060`
Step backwards into a lunge and pull yourself back to standing. Easier on the knees than a
forward lunge and better for beginners.
**P** quadriceps, gluteus maximus · **S** hamstrings, gluteus medius, adductors, core

**6. Dumbbell Reverse Lunge** — `IMG_0061`
Reverse lunge holding dumbbells at the sides. Load added once the bodyweight pattern is clean.
**P** quadriceps, gluteus maximus · **S** hamstrings, gluteus medius, adductors, forearms, core

**7. Stability Ball Wall Squat** — `IMG_0072`
Squat with a stability ball between the lower back and the wall, rolling down and up. Guides the
path and keeps the torso upright.
**P** quadriceps, gluteus maximus · **S** hamstrings, adductors, core

**8. Stability Ball Wall Squat with Dumbbells** — `IMG_0073`
The same wall squat holding dumbbells at the sides for added load.
**P** quadriceps, gluteus maximus · **S** hamstrings, adductors, forearms, core

### Pull — back

**9. Wide-Grip Lat Pulldown** — `IMG_0040 2`
Seated pulldown with a wide overhand grip, bar to the upper chest. Emphasises lat width.
**P** latissimus dorsi · **S** teres major, rhomboids, mid/lower trapezius, posterior deltoid, biceps

**10. Lat Pulldown** — `IMG_0057`
Seated pulldown, bar to the collarbone, torso near-vertical. The general-purpose version.
**P** latissimus dorsi · **S** teres major, rhomboids, trapezius, posterior deltoid, biceps, forearms

**11. Close-Grip Lat Pulldown** — `IMG_0058`
Seated pulldown with a narrow grip, full extension at the top of every rep. Emphasises lat
thickness and lower-lat recruitment.
**P** latissimus dorsi · **S** teres major, rhomboids, mid trapezius, biceps brachii, brachialis

**12. Single-Arm Dumbbell Back Row** ⟲ — `IMG_0069`
One hand braced on a bench, torso bent over, dumbbell drawn from full stretch to the hip.
**Work both sides evenly.**
**P** latissimus dorsi · **S** rhomboids, mid/lower trapezius, teres major, posterior deltoid, biceps, core anti-rotators

**13. Unilateral Cable Lat Pulldown** ⟲ — `IMG_0070`
Tall-kneeling at a high pulley. The bicep drives up to the ear, then the elbow pulls down toward
the ribs to 80–90 degrees. Confirmed by Coach Gui; `IMG_0071` is the same movement from another
angle and is not uploaded. She only works one arm in the clip, so the Notes must say to turn
around and do the other. **Work both sides evenly.**
**P** latissimus dorsi · **S** teres major, rhomboids, lower trapezius, biceps, obliques, glutes

**14. Unilateral Standing Cable Row** ⟲ — `IMG_0067`
Standing at a cable, one arm drawing the handle back past the ribs with the chest held square.
**Work both sides evenly.**
**P** latissimus dorsi · **S** rhomboids, mid/lower trapezius, posterior deltoid, biceps, core anti-rotators

**15. Squat Hold with Cable Row** — `IMG_0066`
Hold a squat and row the cable to the ribs without standing up. Combines a leg isometric with a
horizontal pull.
**P** latissimus dorsi, quadriceps · **S** rhomboids, mid trapezius, biceps, gluteus maximus, core

### Push — chest

**16. Standing Cable Chest Fly** — `IMG_0051`
Standing at a cable crossover, arms opening wide then sweeping together in front of the chest
with a fixed soft elbow.
**P** pectoralis major · **S** anterior deltoid, serratus anterior, biceps (stabilising), core

**18. Dumbbell Chest Fly** — `IMG_0054`
Lying on a flat bench, arms opening wide with a fixed soft elbow, then closing over the chest.
**P** pectoralis major · **S** anterior deltoid, biceps (stabilising), serratus anterior

**19. Incline Dumbbell Chest Press** — `IMG_0056`
Pressing dumbbells from the upper chest on an inclined bench. Shifts emphasis to the upper chest.
**P** pectoralis major (clavicular head) · **S** anterior deltoid, triceps brachii, serratus anterior

**20. Unilateral Dumbbell Yoga Ball Chest Press** ⟲ — `IMG_0050`
Shoulders on a stability ball in a bridge, pressing one dumbbell at a time. The single-sided load
tries to rotate you off the ball, so the core works as hard as the chest. **Work both sides evenly.**
**P** pectoralis major · **S** anterior deltoid, triceps, obliques, gluteus maximus, hamstrings, core anti-rotators

**21. Standing Single-Arm Cable Chest Press** ⟲ — `IMG_0043 2`
Standing side-on to a cable, pressing one arm forward to full extension while resisting rotation.
**Work both sides evenly.**
**P** pectoralis major · **S** anterior deltoid, triceps brachii, serratus anterior, obliques, core

**22. Push-Up** — `IMG_0074`
Full-plank push-up, chest to fist height, body moving as one unit.
**P** pectoralis major · **S** anterior deltoid, triceps brachii, serratus anterior, core, gluteus maximus

### Push — triceps

**23. Bench Dip** — `IMG_0044`
Hands on a bench behind, legs extended, lowering to 90 degrees at the elbow and pressing back up.
**P** triceps brachii · **S** anterior deltoid, pectoralis minor, core

**24. Bench Dip, Knees Bent** — `IMG_0046`
The regressed version with knees bent and feet flat, back brushing the bench.
**P** triceps brachii · **S** anterior deltoid, pectoralis minor

### Core

**25. Cable Chop** ⟲ — `IMG_0065`
Standing at a cable with arms held straight, rotating the weight across the body through the
trunk. The arms only carry the load — the rotation comes from the body. **Work both sides evenly.**
**P** obliques (internal and external) · **S** transverse abdominis, rectus abdominis, erector spinae, glutes, shoulders (isometric)

**26. Stability Ball Crunch** — `IMG_0047`
Lower back supported on a ball, curling up and extending back over the ball for full range.
**P** rectus abdominis · **S** obliques, hip flexors

**27. Stability Ball Leg Raise** — `IMG_0076`
Lying flat, legs raising to meet a ball held overhead, lowering only as far as the back stays flat.
**P** rectus abdominis (lower), hip flexors · **S** obliques, transverse abdominis, adductors

**28. Stability Ball Pass** — `IMG_0078`
Lying flat, passing a ball between hands and feet in a controlled V, no momentum.
**P** rectus abdominis · **S** obliques, hip flexors, transverse abdominis, adductors
