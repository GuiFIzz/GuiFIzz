What GFC Lab offers

GLP-1 weight-management medications

Clinician-evaluated and monitored. We pair them with strength training and protein so you lose fat, not muscle.

Peptide therapy

Clinician-evaluated peptide protocols for recovery, body composition and healthy aging, monitored alongside your training.

Hormone therapy

For men and women with symptoms of low testosterone, perimenopause or menopause, after evaluation and labs.

Metabolic labs

Blood work such as A1c, fasting glucose, lipids and hormones, when ordered by your clinician.

How a visit works

  1. Online intake on Altrohealth's secure platform: health history and goals.
  2. Review by a licensed clinician in your state, with labs if needed.
  3. If appropriate, a prescription sent to a pharmacy and shipped to you.
  4. Ongoing check-ins with the clinician for dosing and safety.
  5. Your coach connects it all to your training, nutrition and Lumen data.

Good to know

  • Prescriptions are issued only when a licensed clinician determines they are medically appropriate. They are never guaranteed.
  • Your medical records stay with Altrohealth on its secure, HIPAA-compliant platform. Your coach sees only what you choose to share.
  • Visits, labs and medications are priced and billed by Altrohealth, separately from coaching.
  • Availability depends on your state.
GFC Lab medical services are provided by independent licensed clinicians through Altrohealth. GFC Xtreme / Metabolic Flex provides coaching only and does not diagnose, prescribe or provide medical care. In an emergency, call 911.
