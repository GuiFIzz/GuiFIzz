## The margin that matters

Fixed costs: $5,950 a month (Founder draw (est.) - your labour lives here, not in variable $4,000, Software stack - CRM, scheduling, email, hosting (est.) $450, Insurance + accounting (est.) $300, Always-on ad spend (est.) $1,200).

- **Break-even: 1 client-months a day.** Below that you lose money every month.
- **Profit margin at your plan** (1 a day): **27%** of every sale, after every cost.
- Capacity: 2.67 a day.
