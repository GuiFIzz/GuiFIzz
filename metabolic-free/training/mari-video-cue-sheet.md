## The cue sheet

### Lower body

**`IMG_0034` · Bodyweight Bench Squat**
- *Breathing:* Inhale as you sit back to the bench. Exhale as you drive through your heels to stand.
- *Tempo:* 3 seconds down, touch the bench lightly, 1 second up. No bouncing off the bench.
- *Mistake:* Don't drop and crash onto the bench — control the last few inches or you lose the whole point.

**`IMG_0037` · Single-Leg Bench Squat**
- *Breathing:* Inhale as you lower to the bench. Exhale hard as you stand on one leg.
- *Tempo:* 3 seconds down, 1 second up. Pause fully seated before each rep.
- *Mistake:* Don't let the working knee cave inward — track it over your middle toe.

**`IMG_0038` · Bodyweight Squat**
- *Breathing:* Inhale on the way down. Exhale as you stand tall.
- *Tempo:* 2 seconds down, 1 second up, chest proud throughout.
- *Mistake:* Don't let your heels lift. If they rise, sit back further instead of down.

**`IMG_0059` · Walking Lunge**
- *Breathing:* Inhale as you step out and sink. Exhale as you drive up and through.
- *Tempo:* 2 seconds down, 1 second up. Steady, not rushed.
- *Mistake:* Don't let the front knee shoot past your toes — step longer, sink straight down.

**`IMG_0060` · Bodyweight Reverse Lunge**
- *Breathing:* Inhale as you step back and lower. Exhale as you pull yourself up.
- *Tempo:* 2 seconds down, 1 second up. Light touch of the back knee, no slam.
- *Mistake:* Don't lean forward. Stack your chest over your hips the whole rep.

**`IMG_0061` · Dumbbell Reverse Lunge**
- *Breathing:* Inhale stepping back. Exhale driving up through the front heel.
- *Tempo:* 3 seconds down, 1 second up. The dumbbells hang relaxed — they're load, not leverage.
- *Mistake:* Don't let the weights swing you forward. Shoulders back, arms dead straight.

**`IMG_0072` · Stability Ball Wall Squat**
- *Breathing:* Inhale as you roll down. Exhale as you press back up the wall.
- *Tempo:* 3 seconds down, 2 second hold at the bottom, 1 second up.
- *Mistake:* Don't stop at a quarter squat — roll down until your thighs are parallel.

**`IMG_0073` · Stability Ball Wall Squat with Dumbbells**
- *Breathing:* Inhale rolling down. Exhale pressing up.
- *Tempo:* 3 seconds down, 1 second up. Add weight only once the bodyweight version is clean.
- *Mistake:* Don't shrug the dumbbells up. Let your arms hang long and keep the work in your legs.

### Pull — the movements the library was missing

**`IMG_0040 2` · Wide-Grip Lat Pulldown**
- *Breathing:* Exhale as you pull the bar to your chest. Inhale as you let it rise.
- *Tempo:* 1 second pull, 1 second squeeze, 3 seconds back up. The way up is where the muscle grows.
- *Mistake:* Don't lean back to haul the bar down. Pull with your back, not your bodyweight.

**`IMG_0041 2` · Lat Pulldown**
- *Breathing:* Exhale pulling down. Inhale on the release.
- *Tempo:* 1 second down, 3 seconds up, under control the whole way.
- *Mistake:* Don't let your shoulders ride up to your ears. Pull them down before the elbows move.

**`IMG_0057` · Lat Pulldown — side view**
- *Breathing:* Exhale as the bar comes to your collarbone. Inhale as it travels back up.
- *Tempo:* 1 second pull, 1 second hold, 3 seconds up.
- *Mistake:* Don't pull the bar behind your neck. In front of the chest, every rep.

**`IMG_0058` · Close-Grip Lat Pulldown**
- *Breathing:* Exhale pulling to your chest. Inhale as you extend back up.
- *Tempo:* 1 second pull, 2 second squeeze, 3 seconds up.
- *Mistake:* Don't stop halfway up. Let your arms straighten fully so your lats get the whole range.

**`IMG_0069` · Single-Arm Dumbbell Row**
- *Breathing:* Exhale as you drive the elbow back to your hip. Inhale as you lower under control.
- *Tempo:* 3 seconds down, 1 second pull, 1 second squeeze at the top.
- *Mistake:* Don't twist your torso to lift the weight. Keep your shoulders square and let the back work.

**`IMG_0070` · Kneeling Single-Arm Cable Pulldown**
- *Breathing:* Exhale as you pull the handle down and across. Inhale as it rises.
- *Tempo:* 1 second pull, 3 seconds back. Stay tall on your knees throughout.
- *Mistake:* Don't let your hips sag or your ribs flare. Squeeze your glutes and stay stacked.

**`IMG_0051` · Standing Cable Reverse Fly**
- *Breathing:* Exhale as your arms sweep apart. Inhale as they return across your body.
- *Tempo:* 2 seconds open, 3 seconds back. This one is never fast.
- *Mistake:* Don't bend your elbows to cheat more weight — go lighter and keep the arms long.

**`IMG_0067` · Standing Single-Arm Cable Reverse Fly**
- *Breathing:* Exhale as the arm sweeps out and back. Inhale as it returns.
- *Tempo:* 2 seconds out, 3 seconds back, no swing.
- *Mistake:* Don't rotate your whole torso with the arm. Chest stays facing forward.

**`IMG_0066` · Squat Hold with Cable Row**
- *Breathing:* Exhale as you row to your ribs. Inhale as the handle travels away.
- *Tempo:* Hold the squat. 1 second row, 3 seconds out. Legs never stop working.
- *Mistake:* Don't stand up as you pull. The squat is the point — stay down for every rep.

### Push

**`IMG_0054` · Dumbbell Chest Fly**
- *Breathing:* Inhale as the arms open wide. Exhale as you bring them together.
- *Tempo:* 3 seconds open, 1 second together, soft bend held in the elbows.
- *Mistake:* Don't drop the dumbbells below shoulder level — that's shoulder strain, not chest.

**`IMG_0056` · Incline Dumbbell Chest Press**
- *Breathing:* Inhale as you lower to your chest. Exhale as you press up.
- *Tempo:* 3 seconds down, 1 second press, stop just short of locking out.
- *Mistake:* Don't clang the dumbbells together at the top. Keep constant tension on the chest.

**`IMG_0074` · Push-Up**
- *Breathing:* Inhale on the way down. Exhale as you press the floor away.
- *Tempo:* 3 seconds down, 1 second up, chest to fist height.
- *Mistake:* Don't let your hips sag. Squeeze your glutes and move your whole body as one plank.

**`IMG_0044` · Bench Dip — legs extended**
- *Breathing:* Inhale as you lower. Exhale as you press back up.
- *Tempo:* 3 seconds down, 1 second up. Elbows track straight back, not out.
- *Mistake:* Don't go so deep your shoulders roll forward — stop at 90 degrees at the elbow.

**`IMG_0046` · Bench Dip — knees bent**
- *Breathing:* Inhale lowering, exhale pressing up.
- *Tempo:* 3 seconds down, 1 second up. This is the easier version — earn the straight legs.
- *Mistake:* Don't drift away from the bench. Keep your back brushing it the whole rep.

**`IMG_0043 2` · Standing Single-Arm Cable Chest Press**
- *Breathing:* Exhale as you press forward. Inhale as the handle returns to your chest.
- *Tempo:* 1 second press, 3 seconds back, resisting the whole return.
- *Mistake:* Don't let the cable twist you open. Brace your core and stay square to the front.

### Core

**`IMG_0047` · Stability Ball Crunch**
- *Breathing:* Exhale as you curl up. Inhale as you lower back over the ball.
- *Tempo:* 2 seconds up, 3 seconds back. Let your back extend over the ball at the bottom.
- *Mistake:* Don't yank on your neck. Keep your chin off your chest and lead with your ribs.

**`IMG_0076` · Stability Ball Leg Raise**
- *Breathing:* Exhale as the legs rise to meet the ball. Inhale as they lower.
- *Tempo:* 2 seconds up, 3 seconds down. Lower only as far as your back stays flat.
- *Mistake:* Don't let your lower back arch off the floor — shorten the range before you lose the brace.

**`IMG_0078` · Stability Ball Pass**
- *Breathing:* Exhale on every pass. Inhale as hands and feet travel back to the floor.
- *Tempo:* 2 seconds up to the pass, 3 seconds back down. Smooth, never thrown.
- *Mistake:* Don't let momentum do it. If your back lifts off the mat, slow down and shorten the reach.

### Ball press trio — names pending your confirmation

**`IMG_0048` · Alternating Dumbbell Chest Press on Stability Ball**
- *Breathing:* Exhale as you press up. Inhale as the dumbbell lowers.
- *Tempo:* 3 seconds down, 1 second press. Alternate without letting the hips drop.
- *Mistake:* Don't let your hips sink. Hold a tabletop bridge from shoulders to knees the whole set.

**`IMG_0049` · Single-Arm Dumbbell Chest Press on Stability Ball**
- *Breathing:* Exhale pressing up. Inhale lowering to your chest.
- *Tempo:* 3 seconds down, 1 second press, pausing at the bottom.
- *Mistake:* Don't let the loaded side rotate you off the ball. Press straight up, hips square.

**`IMG_0050` · Alternating Dumbbell Chest Press on Stability Ball**
- *Breathing:* Exhale on each press. Inhale on each lower.
- *Tempo:* 3 seconds down, 1 second press, one arm at a time.
- *Mistake:* Don't rush the alternation. One controlled arm beats two sloppy ones.

### Cable rotation — names pending your confirmation

**`IMG_0063` · Standing Straight-Arm Cable Pulldown**
- *Breathing:* Exhale as you sweep the handle down to your thighs. Inhale as it rises.
- *Tempo:* 1 second down, 3 seconds back up, arms long throughout.
- *Mistake:* Don't bend your elbows — the moment they bend this becomes a triceps exercise.

**`IMG_0064` · Cable Woodchop, Low to High**
- *Breathing:* Exhale through the rotation. Inhale as you wind back.
- *Tempo:* 1 second through, 3 seconds back. The return is the hard part.
- *Mistake:* Don't let your arms do the chopping. Turn from your hips and ribcage.

**`IMG_0065` · Cable Woodchop**
- *Breathing:* Exhale as you rotate away. Inhale as you return.
- *Tempo:* 1 second through, 3 seconds back, feet planted.
- *Mistake:* Don't let your back foot pivot you around. Rotate through the trunk, not the ankles.

**`IMG_0068` · Kneeling Single-Arm Dumbbell Row**
- *Breathing:* Exhale as you draw the elbow to your hip. Inhale as you lower.
- *Tempo:* 3 seconds down, 1 second pull, 1 second squeeze.
- *Mistake:* Don't round your back. Flat spine from tailbone to head, every single rep.

**`IMG_0071` · Kneeling Single-Arm Cable Pulldown**
- *Breathing:* Exhale on the pull. Inhale on the return.
- *Tempo:* 1 second pull, 3 seconds back, torso still.
- *Mistake:* Don't rock backwards to move the weight. If you're swaying, drop the pin a plate.
