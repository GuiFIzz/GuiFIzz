# Organic content system

## Coach batch day (1 hour/week, or 2 hours every other week)
Record 4–6 raw phone videos, 30–60 s each, from the prompts below. The Content agent turns each into a
reel/short, 1–2 carousels, a story set, a LinkedIn post and an email snippet. **One video → ~8 assets.**

**Prompt bank (rotate):**
1. "The biggest mistake I see people make on GLP-1s is…"
2. "Here's what my Lumen said this morning, and what I'm eating because of it."
3. "If your doctor said 'pre-diabetic', do these 3 things this week."
4. "Why I'd rather you lift than run if you're over 45."
5. Client win (with consent): "{{name}} came to me with…"
6. "Myth: you have to eat less forever."
7. "What a biweekly check-in actually looks like." (screen-recorded session, with consent)
8. "Every tool humanity built, used in the right order: here's the order."

## Weekly calendar (auto-scheduled)

| Day | Pillar | Format | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | **Muscle** (lifting, protein, GLP-1 muscle loss) | Reel from coach video | Quiz link in bio |
| Tue | **Blood sugar / Power Warrior** | Carousel: "3 signs your blood sugar is swinging" | Comment "SCORE" → auto-DM quiz link |
| Wed | **Lumen / meal timing** | Coach's own Lumen reading + meal | Story poll |
| Thu | **Proof** | Client win / testimonial | Book a call |
| Fri | **Tools → behavior** | Myth-busting reel | Quiz |
| Sat | Behind the scenes / personal | Story | — |
| Sun | Weekly tip email repurposed | Carousel | Newsletter sign-up |

**Comment-to-DM automation:** "Comment SCORE" posts trigger a DM with the quiz link (ManyChat or GHL).
It's one of the highest-converting organic tactics right now.

**Platforms, by priority:** Instagram + Facebook (the audience is 35–65) → YouTube Shorts → TikTok → LinkedIn (B2B).
