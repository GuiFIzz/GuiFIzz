The 7-Day
Metabolic Reset

Seven days of eating in the order your body actually handles best — protein first, muscle protected, blood sugar steadier. No crash diet, no shopping list of things you've never heard of. Just the habits we build with every client, starting today.

Before you start: what this is

This isn't a diet you "go on" and then come off. Seven days isn't long enough to transform your body — and anyone who tells you otherwise is selling something. What seven days is long enough for is to install the handful of habits that actually move your metabolism, and to feel the difference enough to keep them.

Everything here is built on the same research behind our coaching program (see the studies). Three ideas drive all of it:

Muscle is your metabolic organ. Roughly 80% of the sugar in your blood after a meal gets taken up by skeletal muscle. Protect muscle and you protect your blood sugar.
The order you eat in changes the response. Eating vegetables and protein before carbohydrates produces meaningfully lower post-meal glucose and insulin than the identical meal eaten carbs-first.
Walking right after you eat works. A 10-minute walk after a meal lowers post-meal blood sugar more than one longer walk at some other time of day — the effect is biggest after dinner.

The four daily non-negotiables

Do these every single day for seven days. Everything else is detail.

1
Protein at every meal

Aim for roughly 0.7–1 gram of protein per pound of your goal bodyweight per day, split across your meals. Start with breakfast — it's the meal most people skip protein at entirely.

2
Vegetables and protein first, carbs last

Same food, same plate, different order. Eat your vegetables and your protein, then your rice, bread, pasta or potato at the end of the meal.

3
A 10-minute walk after your biggest meal

Not a workout. A walk. Ten minutes, starting within about half an hour of finishing eating.

4
Seven hours of sleep, protected

Sleep is metabolic. Restricting it measurably worsens how your body handles glucose — you can do everything else right and lose ground here.

How to build every plate

FirstVegetables — a real portion, not a garnish. Whatever you'll actually eat.
SecondProtein — meat, fish, eggs, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, tofu, legumes.
LastCarbohydrate — rice, potato, bread, pasta, fruit. Not banned. Just last.

Nothing is forbidden on this reset. You're changing sequence and emphasis, not eliminating food groups. That's exactly why it's still doable on day 30, day 90 and day 300.

Your seven days

One new focus a day, stacked on top of the last. By day seven you're doing all of them.

DayWhat you add
Day 1The walk Change nothing about your food today. Just walk for 10 minutes after your biggest meal. One habit, zero friction.
Day 2Protein at breakfast Keep the walk. Add a real protein source to your first meal — eggs, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, a shake. Notice how the late-morning hunger behaves.
Day 3Meal order Keep both. Today, at every meal, eat vegetables and protein before you touch the carbohydrate on the plate.
Day 4Hit your protein number Add up what you're actually eating for the first time. Most people are far under. Adjust dinner to close the gap.
Day 5Sleep window Pick a lights-out time that gives you seven hours and defend it. Phone out of the room if that's what it takes.
Day 6Move something heavy One strength session. Two movements is enough: a squat pattern and a row or hinge pattern. Thirty minutes.
Day 7Review, don't restart Which of the six stuck without effort? Which needed willpower? The ones that needed willpower are the ones worth getting help with.
